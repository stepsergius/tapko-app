Thank you for buying Tapko Pro!

Your license key is on the order page and in the receipt email.

Activate Pro in three steps

  • Copy your license key from the receipt email.
  • Open Tapko's settings: click the Tapko icon in the menu bar and choose Settings, then open About.
  • Paste the key and click Activate. All your gestures work right away.

Don't have Tapko yet?

Download Tapko for Mac. It needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Using more than one Mac?

Your key works on up to 3 Macs. To move it to another Mac, click Deactivate on This Mac in Tapko's settings, under About.
